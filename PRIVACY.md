# Giffer Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-04-26_

## Summary

Giffer does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything happens on your device.

## What Giffer accesses

- **Photos library**: Giffer uses Apple's standard Photos picker (`PHPickerViewController`) to let you choose a Live Photo. The system handles this picker out-of-process; Giffer never gains access to your full Photos library and does not receive a Photos permission grant.

## What Giffer stores

Nothing persistent. Giffer does not save a history of which Live Photos you have opened, your editing settings, or any analytics. When you quit the app, no record of your activity remains beyond standard iOS system caches.

## What Giffer transmits

Nothing. Giffer has no network code, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no third-party SDKs.

## Third parties

There are none. Giffer does not embed any third-party libraries or services.

## Children's privacy

Giffer is suitable for all ages. Because Giffer collects no data, no special handling for children's information is required.
